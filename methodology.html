How We Score Cities: Methodology

Every city receives a LiveScore from 0–100, a weighted blend of 12 factor scores. Each factor is also shown as a letter grade from A+ to D.

Weights

FactorWeightWhat it captures
Safety16%Crime and personal safety perception
Affordability16%50% absolute monthly cost (for remote/foreign earners) + 50% local salary vs cost
Healthcare14%Quality and accessibility of care
Jobs & career12%Job market depth and salaries
Nature & outdoors9%Access to parks, coast, mountains
Culture & food9%Arts, dining, events, heritage
Family-friendly8%Schools, childcare, green space, safety for kids
Walkability & transit6%Car-free living potential
Air quality5%Average pollution exposure
Internet5%Typical fixed-broadband speed (log-scaled)

Nightlife and English-friendliness are shown on report cards and used in the City Match quiz, but they don't count toward the LiveScore.

Data sources & status

Scores are editorial indices that combine public indicators (national statistics offices, OECD, WHO air-quality data, broadband speed tests, crowd-sourced price indices) with editorial review. All cost and salary figures are indicative estimates meant for comparing cities, not quotes. Always check current local prices before you make a financial decision.

Updates & corrections

We review the dataset at least once a year and after major events. Spotted something off? Send a correction. We fix confirmed errors quickly.

Independence

Advertisers, sponsors, partners and donors cannot influence scores or rankings.
