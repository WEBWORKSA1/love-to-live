Calculator FAQ

How is the cost-of-living comparison calculated?
We compare each city's cost index (New York = 100) and central 1-bed rent. Your income is split into roughly 35% housing and 65% everything else, and each part is scaled by the matching index. Household size adjusts the non-rent basket.
How accurate is the moving cost estimate?
It gives a planning range based on typical rates for distance, volume and services. Real quotes depend on inventory, access (stairs, elevators), dates and insurance. Get binding quotes to be sure — compare free quotes here.
What is a safe mortgage payment?
A common lender guideline is the 28/36 rule: keep housing costs under 28% of gross income and total debt payments under 36%. The calculator flags where you land.
