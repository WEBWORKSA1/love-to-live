Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This template should be reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, move details and messages submitted in forms.
  • Automatically: device and usage data through cookies and similar technologies (analytics and advertising), only with your consent where required.

How we use it

  • To respond to your requests and connect you with partners for the service you select (for example movers for a moving-quote request).
  • To send newsletters you signed up for (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To run contests, review job applications and improve the site.
  • To show ads, including personalised ads if you consent.

Sharing

We share quote-request details only with the matched partners for the service you chose, and with service providers that help us operate (form processing, email, analytics, advertising). We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA/Québec Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to processing. Contact us via the contact page.

Retention & security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we protect it with reasonable technical and organisational measures.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16.
