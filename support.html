Supporter FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?
LoveToLive is an independent publisher, not a registered charity, so contributions are generally not tax-deductible. They go directly into running and growing the site.
Can I cancel monthly support?
Yes, anytime, from the receipt email or by contacting us. No questions asked.
Do supporters influence rankings?
Never. Rankings are editorially independent and data-driven. Supporters and sponsors cannot buy a better score. See our methodology.
