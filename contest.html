Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction), except where prohibited by law. Employees of LoveToLive and sponsors are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: from launch until the deadline shown on the countdown (23:59 UTC). One entry per person per category.
  4. Judging: a panel scores entries on creativity (40%), storytelling/emotion (30%) and how well the entry captures daily life (30%). People's Choice is decided by public vote, with fraud checks.
  5. Prizes: as listed above; prizes are non-transferable, and winners are responsible for any taxes. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days.
  6. Rights: you keep ownership of your work. You grant LoveToLive a non-exclusive licence to display it on our site, newsletter and social channels with credit.
  7. Privacy: entry data is handled per our Privacy Policy.
  8. Skill-based contest: winners are chosen on merit, not by chance. Where required by law, a skill-testing question may be asked (e.g. in Canada).

Full official rules are available on request. Void where prohibited.
