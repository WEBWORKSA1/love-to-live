About LoveToLive

We believe where you live shapes how you live. LoveToLive exists to make one of life's biggest decisions easier, more honest and more joyful.

What we do

  • Discover: transparent rankings and report cards for cities worldwide.
  • Decide: free tools — City Match quiz, cost-of-living, moving and mortgage calculators, side-by-side comparisons.
  • Move: a single, privacy-respecting form to compare vetted movers, agents, lenders and relocation specialists.
  • Live well: guides on community, health, money and happiness in your new home.

How we stay independent

We earn money from advertising, partner introductions, sponsorships and reader support. None of these can buy a better score. Our rankings follow a published methodology, and every commercial relationship is labelled (see our disclosure).
