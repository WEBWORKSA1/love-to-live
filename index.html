FAQ

Questions, answered

How do you rank the best places to live?
Each city gets a 0–100 LiveScore that blends 12 factors — safety, healthcare, affordability, jobs, nature, culture, family-friendliness, walkability, air quality, internet and more. You can re-weight everything to your own priorities in the City Match quiz. Full details on our methodology page.
Is LoveToLive free?
Yes. Our tools and guides are free. We earn from ads, from partners who pay for introductions when you request quotes, and from reader support.
Who are your moving, real-estate and mortgage partners?
Licensed, insured and reviewed companies in each market. You choose which services you want to hear from, and nothing is shared without your consent.
How accurate is the cost of living data?
Figures are indicative estimates meant for comparing cities, and they are updated regularly. Always check current rents and prices locally before you sign a lease or a job offer.
