A $120K offer in one city can leave you poorer than a $90K offer in another. Here's how to compare fairly.

1. Compare take-home, not gross

Income tax (national, state or provincial, and local), social contributions and mandatory insurance vary a lot. Estimate net monthly pay for each offer.

2. Price the home you'd actually rent

Look up real listings for the size and neighbourhood you want. Housing is the biggest swing factor between cities.

3. Adjust everything else

Use the cost-of-living calculator to scale food, transport, utilities and leisure. Add costs specific to your life: childcare, a car if the city is car-dependent, and flights home.

4. Weigh career upside

A deeper job market means more future options. Count promotions, network effects and remote flexibility, not just year-one pay.

5. Put a price on quality of life

Commute time, climate, safety and proximity to people you love have real value. Our city comparison helps you see the trade-offs side by side.
