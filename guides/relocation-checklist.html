Moving is a project, and projects succeed with a plan. Work backwards from move day using this checklist. Your ticks are saved in this browser, and you can print it any time.

90–60 days out: decide & budget

  • Shortlist 2–3 destination cities (try the City Match quiz).
  • Compare cost of living and the salary you'll need (calculator).
  • Set a total moving budget with a 10–15% buffer.
  • Visit if you can — spend at least one weekday and one weekend there.
  • Research neighbourhoods: commute, schools, noise, safety, walkability.
  • Check visa/residency requirements if moving abroad.
  • Start decluttering: sell, donate or recycle anything you won't miss.

60–30 days out: book & secure

  • Get at least 3 in-home or video moving estimates (compare free quotes).
  • Verify each mover's licence (US interstate: USDOT number on FMCSA's registry) and reviews.
  • Book your mover or container and get the estimate in writing.
  • Secure housing: sign a lease or schedule the home closing.
  • Give notice to your landlord (check your lease's notice period).
  • Request school and medical records.
  • Start using up food in the freezer and pantry.

30–14 days out: admin

  • Set up mail forwarding and update your address with banks, employer and subscriptions.
  • Schedule utility and internet shut-off at the old place and set-up at the new one.
  • Update insurance: home/renters, auto, health.
  • Arrange pet transport and a vet health certificate if needed.
  • Pack non-essentials room by room, labelling each box with room and contents.
  • Photograph valuables and electronics set-ups for insurance and reassembly.

14–1 days out: final prep

  • Confirm date, time, address and access details with your mover.
  • Pack an "open first" box: chargers, toiletries, medications, documents, bedding, kettle.
  • Defrost the fridge and drain hoses and fuel from equipment.
  • Reserve elevator or parking permits at both ends if required.
  • Withdraw some cash for tips and incidentals.

Move day

  • Walk the crew through the home, pointing out fragile items.
  • Check the inventory list and bill of lading before you sign.
  • Do a final sweep: cupboards, closets, garage, meters (take photos).
  • Hand over keys and take photos of the empty home for your deposit.

First 30 days: live well

  • Inspect deliveries and file any damage claims promptly (deadlines apply).
  • Register your residency, driver's licence and vehicle as locally required.
  • Find a doctor, dentist and pharmacy.
  • Join two local groups: a sport or hobby club, and a neighbourhood group.
  • Find your "third place": a café, park or library you visit weekly.
