Moving to another country is exciting and admin-heavy. Tackle the steps in this order to avoid expensive mistakes.

1. Secure your legal right to live there

Everything depends on your visa or residence permit. Common routes include work sponsorship, digital-nomad or remote-work visas, retirement or passive-income visas, study, family reunification and investor routes. Rules change often, so always confirm on the destination's official immigration website or with a licensed immigration adviser.

2. Plan money & taxes

  • Understand tax residency rules in both countries, and whether a tax treaty applies.
  • Open a multi-currency account to cut FX fees.
  • Budget 3–6 months of living costs plus deposits (often 1–3 months' rent).

3. Arrange healthcare

Find out whether you'll qualify for the public system and when. Until then, get international health insurance that covers pre-existing conditions if you need it.

4. Decide what to ship

International shipping is priced by volume and route. Many people ship a few boxes by air and sell furniture instead. Compare international movers for a full container or shared (LCL) load.

5. Land softly

  • Book a furnished short-term rental for your first 1–3 months while you scout neighbourhoods.
  • Register your address, get a local tax ID, open a bank account and get a SIM card.
  • Start language lessons in week one, because it's the fastest route to belonging.

6. Build a life

Say yes to invitations for the first six months. Join a club, volunteer and find your regular café. The expats who thrive are the ones who invest locally.
