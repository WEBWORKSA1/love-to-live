The best first base is not the most famous one. It's the one with reliable internet, a legal way to stay, a workable time zone and a community to plug into.

The five criteria that matter

  1. Internet: 50+ Mbps with a backup (a mobile data plan or a coworking space).
  2. Legal stay: tourist-visa days vs a digital-nomad visa. Know the rules for working remotely.
  3. Time zone: overlap of at least 3–4 hours with your team or clients.
  4. Cost: a budget that lets you save, not just survive.
  5. Community: coworking spaces, meetups and fellow nomads.

Filter cities by these in our nomad rankings.

Your nomad set-up

  • Travel-medical or international health insurance.
  • A laptop stand, noise-cancelling headphones and a universal adapter.
  • A VPN for public Wi-Fi.
  • A multi-currency card with low FX fees.
  • Cloud backup of all documents.

Slow down

Staying 1–3 months per base lowers costs (monthly rental discounts), improves productivity and turns acquaintances into friends. Burnout usually comes from moving too fast.

Don't ignore taxes

Being "location-independent" doesn't make you tax-independent. Find out where you're tax-resident and get professional advice if you earn across borders.
