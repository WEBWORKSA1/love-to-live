Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

LoveToLive is free to use. To keep it that way we earn revenue in these ways:

  • Advertising (e.g. Google AdSense and direct-sold placements), always labelled "Advertisement" or "Sponsored".
  • Partner referrals: when you request quotes, we may be paid by the movers, agents, lenders, insurers or relocation companies we connect you with.
  • Affiliate links: some links to products and services earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Sponsorships of guides, videos, newsletters and contests, always clearly marked.
  • Reader support through donations and memberships.

Compensation never affects our city scores or rankings. Where US law applies, this disclosure is made in line with FTC guidance on endorsements.
